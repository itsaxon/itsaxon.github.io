Navigation adapted from the official landing navbar: https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/tree/main/src/components/landingnew/Navbar . Geometry, scroll threshold, glass styling and hover behavior ported to TypeScript with Motion and local site branding.

Dot Grid TS-TW source: https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/src/ts-tailwind/Backgrounds/DotGrid/DotGrid.tsx . Adapted for dense paper-colored dots, reduced motion, visible-only drawing and scoped pointer interactions.

Magnet Lines TS-TW: https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/src/ts-tailwind/Animations/MagnetLines/MagnetLines.tsx . Adapted with reduced-motion support and larger responsive artwork.

Dither Veil TS-TW: https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/src/ts-tailwind/Animations/DitherVeil/DitherVeil.tsx . Local SVG artwork and site palette; powered by OGL.

Particle Text TS-TW: https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/src/ts-tailwind/TextAnimations/ParticleText/ParticleText.tsx . Adapted for two-line Chinese headings and denser, left-aligned particles. Dither Veil matte made transparent to reveal the static page dot field.

Tech Text and Specular Button TS-TW: https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/tree/main/src/ts-tailwind . Adapted for Chinese headings, local palette, accessible heading text and reading navigation.
